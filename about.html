Our Story

Why We Started MailFlow

Marketers used to juggle one tool for newsletters, another for transactional receipts, and fragile spreadsheets for segmentation. We built MailFlow to unify campaign design, behavioral workflows, and real-time analytics into a single fast interface.

Every release is guided by three principles: zero bloat, transparent deliverability telemetry, and inclusive design across Light/Dark themes and LTR/RTL languages.
